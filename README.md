# not-loom

Record demo videos of your screen with your camera in a round bubble, a live audio meter so you know your mic is picking you up, and a Stop button you can't miss. Everything stays on your machine.

There are two versions:

- **[`mac/`](mac/README.md): native Mac menu bar app** (Swift, macOS 15+). Recommended. The menu bar item turns into the Stop button while you record, and you can trim the start and end after you stop.
- **[`web/`](web/README.md): browser version** (Chrome). No install; run `npm start` in `web/`.
